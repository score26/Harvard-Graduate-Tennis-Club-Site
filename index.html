Getting started is simple.

  1. Fill out the form

    Join the email list and get notified when we release spots at the beginning of each semester.

  2. Become a member

    Pay the $30/semester membership fee through our Google Form once notified.

  3. Attend the sessions

    We release the Google sign-up sheet every week, usually a day before practice. Spots are limited by court availability, so act quickly.
