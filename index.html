Harvard Graduate Tennis Club

A tennis community for Harvard graduate students, on and off the court.

Since the summer of 2024, we have been fostering a vibrant community among Harvard graduate students through a shared passion for tennis. Today, we are the largest graduate club on campus.

Getting started is simple.

01

Fill out the form

Join the email list and get notified when we release spots at the beginning of each semester.

02

Become a member

Pay the $30/semester membership fee through our Google Form once notified.

03

Attend the sessions

We release the Google sign-up sheet every week (usually a day before the practice session). Spots are limited due to court availability, so act quickly!
